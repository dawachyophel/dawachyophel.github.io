Multimodal neuroimaging combining structural MRI and PET captures complementary aspects of Alzheimer's disease, yet most AI systems output a single label without showing which modality drove the decision. This work introduces the Modality Contribution Network (MCNet) and the Modality Contribution Score (MCS), a per-patient, ablation-based attribution that sums to one for every subject.
