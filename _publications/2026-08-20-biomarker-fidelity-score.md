The Biomarker Fidelity Score (BFS) measures the spatial overlap between individual-level 3D explainability attention maps and atlas-registered Alzheimer's-relevant regions of interest. Five explainability methods were benchmarked across three volumetric architectures on ADNI-3 and externally replicated on OASIS-3.
